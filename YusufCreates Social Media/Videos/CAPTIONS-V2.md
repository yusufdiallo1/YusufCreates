## `v-04-flat-pages`

> Four pages or nine pages — same price.
>
> Three pages is $750. Four through nine is $950, flat.
>
> The old way charged per page above three, so nine pages came to more than a
> web app, which is absurd. Worse, it made the price unpredictable: adding one
> page mid-project started a conversation about money instead of a decision
> about the site.
>
> Most of the cost is design, build setup and deployment — none of which scale
> with page count. The ninth page genuinely isn't much more work than the fifth.

## Posting order

Lead with the strongest hook, not the cheapest product. `v-02` is the one most
likely to get shared — publishing a full price list is genuinely unusual and
people screenshot it.
